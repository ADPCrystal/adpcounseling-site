Crystal W. Hamilton, LMHC

Founder and Director of Clinical Services

Since 2004, Crystal has worked in the behavioral health field in a variety of settings, first with children and then with adult and older adult populations. She has worked in the Residential Support Waiver Behavior Support Program as a clinician, then supervisor at a community mental health agency, and contracted with a skilled nursing facility in private practice before forming A Different Path Counseling, PLLC as a behavioral health agency to serve participants in Snohomish County.

In addition to being Clinical Director, Crystal provides individual and couples therapy to adults in person and via telehealth. In therapy, we will work together to help you find relief and overcome obstacles in your life. A therapeutic approach is chosen by what will suit you best. Modalities used in session include Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Lifespan Integration, Gottman Method, and Emotion Freedom Techniques/Tapping. Crystal has a passion for working with couples affected by substance use disorders and codependency. Somatic approaches are used to address attachment injuries and past trauma. Crystal specializes in Religious Trauma/Spiritual Abuse and has a secular stance in the therapeutic relationship.

Programs

Residential Service Waiver (RSW) Behavior Support Program

What is RSW? RSW Behavior Support is a DSHS program that supports adults living in adult family homes who have combined complex medical and behavioral health issues. It helps participants meet their needs, reduce future hospitalizations, and live safely in the community. ADPC staff also work closely with caregivers to model, skill-train and create strategies to manage behavioral issues that affect housing stability.

Who do we serve? ADPC provides services in Snohomish and North King Counties. We are contracted with DSHS, which refers people discharging from a psychiatric hospital into an adult family home.

Benefits. Participants receive individual behavior management support and take part in community activities. With ADPC staff, they practice communication skills, build healthy relationships and integrate into the community, and develop coping skills to help prevent future psychiatric and/or medical hospitalizations.

Our mission. We believe in the value of every person we work with. We offer compassion, support and life enrichment, with a person-first, trauma-informed approach, meeting participants where they are at.

Peer Support

Wherever you are at, you are not alone. A peer counselor is available to listen to you in a non-judgmental, open-minded way and to support you in discovering your strengths, developing a support network, setting goals, and self-advocacy.

  • Wellness Circle: in-person monthly workshop at your facility to help participants maintain an active lifestyle.
  • Group: in-person small groups at a facility to process life's obstacles and learn from each other.
  • Individual: sessions in person or by telehealth to help meet your self-identified goals.
  • Text/Email: online support for people with busy schedules or who prefer it.
